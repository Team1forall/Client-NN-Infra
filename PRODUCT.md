# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router, TypeScript), hand-written CSS Modules, GSAP for scroll choreography. Chosen by the user. Enquiries post to a Google Sheet via a Google Apps Script web app (user's choice).

## Users

Prospective buyers, investors and families in and around Bengaluru who have heard about NN Infra's upcoming Project N and want to know what it is, whether the developer can be trusted, and how to register interest. Many arrive on phones from a shared link or a QR code.

## Product Purpose

The NN Infra website announces Project N (Bengaluru, January 2027) and turns curiosity into registered enquiries, using the company's real four-decade history (since 1986) as proof of credibility.

## Positioning

A land-first developer with a real lineage: Nethravathi Group was founded in 1986 by Mr. B. K. Nagabhushana Reddy on the belief that everyone deserves a piece of land they can call their own. It is now led into its next generation by Mr. Jayanth N (Chairman and Director) with a modern approach to design, sustainability and lifestyle.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Project N facts confirmed so far: name, city (Bengaluru), announcement timing (January 2027), and that the supplied material refers to limited fractional ownership opportunities. Everything else is undecided and must be shown as "To be announced".
- No pricing, returns, appreciation, liquidity, approvals, specifications or architecture may be stated or implied.
- Office address (confirmed): 270/4, 1st Cross Road, Krishna Reddy Layout, Bengaluru 560068, Karnataka, India.
- Phone, email and WhatsApp details are not yet supplied: marked placeholders in `src/content/site.ts`.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: NN Infra, a Nethravathi Group venture. Tagline: "NN Infra | Legacy. Design. Delivery."
- Creative north star: "A legacy rooted in land. A vision designed for what comes next."
- Palette from the brief: soft sage #E9EEDF, botanical #B9C9AF, forest #35483A, ivory #F7F5EE, stone #E6E0D5, charcoal #292D29.
- Nethravathi Group logo supplied (`logo.pdf`): three ascending green bars with "NETHRAVATHI GROUP". No NN Infra logo has been supplied yet.

## Evidence on Hand

- Past work (names only, no dates or photos supplied): Krishnappa Reddy Layout, Sai Layout, Sri Rama Layout, "and more"; more than one million square feet of commercial development.
- No photographs, videos, portraits or Project N renders were supplied. Imagery is licensed stock or AI-generated stand-ins, labelled representative. Never fabricate testimonials, awards, approvals or figures.

## Product Principles

1. Project N first: the announcement is legible within one viewport.
2. Prove, don't claim: credibility comes from names, dates and figures the company actually has.
3. Honest unknowns: undecided details are designed as "To be announced", never guessed.
4. The enquiry path is never more than one tap away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced-motion support, accessible form validation. Mobile-first performance for Indian mobile networks.
